## About

I am a climate scientist based in Hamburg, Germany, currently completing my MSc in Integrated Climate System Sciences at Universität Hamburg. My work sits at the intersection of atmospheric modelling, high-performance computing, and scientific software development.

I believe science is the most honest framework for understanding the world — and that the best research happens when rigour meets curiosity. I approach model development not just as a technical exercise, but as a way to interrogate the physical systems we are still learning to describe.

Currently, I am contributing to mesospheric model development at the **Leibniz-Institut für Atmosphärenphysik (IAP)**, working on the documentation, testing, and Fortran → Julia transition of the **MIMAS** model. I also work with **UA-ICON** on HPC systems, and increasingly integrate AI tooling into my scientific workflows.

---

## Current Work

### 🧊 MIMAS Model Development · *IAP Kühlungsborn*
Contributing to the structured documentation and codebase modernisation of the MIMAS mesospheric model. This includes evaluating the Fortran → Julia port, improving code readability, and building developer guides to support long-term maintainability and onboarding.

### 🤖 Nisha · *LLM Research Assistant for IAP*
An LLM-powered conversational agent designed to help researchers and new team members understand the MIMAS model — its scientific background, Fortran code structure, and physical parameterisations. Also supports task logging and research organisation. Deployed internally at IAP.

### 🎓 ApplicationGo · *European MSc Project Discovery Agent*
An LLM-based agent that helps prospective postgraduate students discover and filter MSc research projects across European institutions, and guides them through preparing application materials — reducing information overload and improving targeting.
