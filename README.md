## About

I am a climate scientist based in Hamburg, Germany, currently completing my MSc in Integrated Climate System Sciences at Universität Hamburg. My work focuses on **atmospheric modelling, high-performance computing, and scientific software development**, with an emphasis on understanding transport processes and mesospheric dynamics.

I approach modelling as both a computational and scientific problem — building systems that not only run efficiently, but also help interrogate the physical processes we are still learning to describe.

Currently, I work at the **Leibniz-Institut für Atmosphärenphysik (IAP)** on mesospheric model development (MIMAS), HPC-based simulations (UA-ICON), and increasingly on integrating **AI-driven tools into scientific workflows**.

---

## Current Work

### 🧊 MIMAS Model Development · *IAP Kühlungsborn*
Working on documentation, validation, and modernisation of the **MIMAS mesospheric model**, including the **Fortran → Julia transition**. Focused on improving model transparency, reproducibility, and long-term maintainability.

---

### 🤖 SciComm.AI · *AI Research Workspace (DESY Start-up School)*
Co-developing an **AI-assisted platform for scientific literature analysis and communication**, designed to convert research PDFs into structured, explainable outputs.

Core work includes:
- Building a **modular backend pipeline** for PDF parsing (text, images, sections)
- Developing workflows for **scientific claim extraction, equation referencing, and structured content generation**
- Designing a **FastAPI-based architecture** for scalable document processing

Goal: Bridge the gap between **scientific knowledge and accessibility**, enabling faster and more structured research workflows.

---

### 🤖 Nisha · *LLM Research Assistant for IAP*
An LLM-powered assistant for exploring **MIMAS model physics, code structure, and simulations**, supporting onboarding, knowledge retrieval, and research organisation within the group.

---

### 🎓 ApplicationGo · *MSc Discovery & Application Agent*
An AI-based system for discovering and filtering **European MSc research opportunities**, and assisting with CV/SOP preparation to improve application targeting.
